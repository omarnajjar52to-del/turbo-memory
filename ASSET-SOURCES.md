# Image and content provenance — version 23

- `showroom-campaign.webp`: AI-generated conceptual storefront inspired by the user-supplied promotional image. The image contains no signage or text and is not a documentary photograph of the actual shop. The overlaid advertising text is editable HTML.
- `hero-ac-cutout.webp`: AI-assisted transparent extraction from a user-provided product image.
- `hero-fridge-cutout.webp`, `hero-washer-cutout.webp`: AI-assisted transparent product illustrations. Exact model appearance is not verified. Interior variants are illustrative.
- `product-tv-cutout.webp`: transparent AI-assisted illustration derived from a product promotional image; exact model appearance and commercial reuse rights are not established.
- `lg-*-cutout.webp`: transparent generated illustrations for the six refrigerator/freezer entries. They are not photographs establishing an exact model match.
- `lg-*-label-readable.svg`: readable transcriptions of specifications from the user-supplied appliance labels. Original photos and serial numbers are omitted at the user's request.

Replace illustrative appliance renderings with showroom-owned, licensed and model-matched photos when available. The showroom is an independent seller of LG products, not the official LG website. Product availability is not connected to a live inventory system.

- `assets/lg-brand-logo.webp` — LG Global brand assets, Heritage Red / White logo for dark backgrounds. https://www.lg.com/global/our-identity/brand-assets/

- `collection-microwave.webp`: LG NeoChef product image from the LMC0975 specification sheet, used to illustrate the kitchen appliances category. It does not indicate showroom stock. https://www.lg.com/us/support/products/documents/LMC0975-Spec-Sheet.pdf

- The 18 interior views and their thumbnails are cinematic edits of the supplied showroom photographs, presented in the homepage gallery. The gallery retains distinct views of the televisions, refrigerators, washers, kitchen displays, floor-care devices and showroom aisles.
